About CampusBite

CampusBite is a college canteen management system designed to make campus food ordering simple, fast and convenient for students, faculty and canteen kitchen staff.

For Students & Faculty

  • No standing in long queues during 15-minute lecture breaks.
  • Real-time visibility on dish availability and out-of-stock items.
  • Digital tracking ticket so you know exactly when food is ready.

For Canteen Administration

  • Streamlined digital order management without paper mess.
  • Instant menu updates and inventory status controls.
  • Sales analytics and statistical reports on daily revenue.
